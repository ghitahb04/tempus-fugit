TO GABE WITH AFFECTION, OBVIOUSLY

My dear Gabe,

Happy birthday. I hope this next chapter brings you beautiful places, impossible ideas that somehow become real, good food, long evenings, and plenty of reasons to smile.

There's something rather lovely about the way you see the world — as if there's always another detail worth noticing, another line worth drawing, another story hidden in the things everyone else walks past.

I hope you never lose that. And I hope, among all the things you make and all the places you go, you remember to make a little room for yourself, too.

I'm very glad our paths crossed. Don't make me repeat that; it was embarrassing enough the first time.

Yours (unfortunately for you),
Viper ♡
